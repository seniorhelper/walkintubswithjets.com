Free written quote

Get a free, no-pressure quote for a walk-in tub with jets

How it works: send the short form or call 1-888-779-2284. A specialist from Aging Safely Baths will contact you, usually within one business day, to ask about your bathroom and what you want the jets to do. You get a written quote. There is no obligation, no deadline and no “today only” price.

Request your quote

Your info is private and never sold. By submitting you agree Aging Safely Baths may contact you regarding your inquiry.

A BBB Accredited Business. Tap the seal to read our BBB listing before you decide anything. We believe we offer real quality and value, and we would rather you check than take our word for it.

What happens next

  1. A friendly call. We ask about your bathroom size, who will bathe, which joints ache, and whether you prefer air, water or both kinds of jets.
  2. A written quote. Itemized, for your exact situation. Take as long as you like to think it over.
  3. Installation, if you go ahead. Installation often takes just one day, by factory-certified installers. Supply-only shipping is also available.

What to have handy (it helps, but it is not required)

  • A rough measurement of your current tub or alcove: length and width.
  • Which side the drain is on, as you face the tub.
  • A photo of the bathroom and, if easy, of your electrical panel. Jetted tubs need dedicated circuits; see our electrical guide.
  • Your water heater size in gallons (it is on the label).
  • Anything about health or mobility that matters: knees, hips, balance, a wheelchair, a caregiver who helps.
  • If you are a veteran, whether you have a service-connected disability rating. VA HISA grants may help.

Questions people ask

Is the quote really free?

Yes. There is no charge for the call or the written quote, and no obligation to buy.

Do you install nationwide?

Aging Safely Baths offers installation across the U.S. and supply-only shipping, including to Canada. Tell us your zip code and we will explain the options for your area.

Can my son or daughter request the quote for me?

Of course. Many requests come from adult children. Put their phone number on the form, and mention who will be using the tub.
